My research is on **quantum chemistry with quantum computers**:

- variational quantum eigensolvers (VQE) for molecules,
- quantum embedding: density matrix embedding theory (DMET) and projection-based embedding,
- reducing measurement cost, e.g. grouping commuting Pauli strings and factorised Hamiltonians.

More write-ups will appear here and on the [blog](/posts/).
